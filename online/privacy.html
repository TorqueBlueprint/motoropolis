Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

The short version

  • You sign in with Google so we can see which YouTube channel you are. We read it once, at sign-in, with read-only access.
  • We keep your channel name and picture, a one-way fingerprint of your channel ID (not the ID itself) and your game data (balance, money history, job, salary days). Our sign-in provider keeps your Google email.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling, no sharing of your data. It is stored with Supabase in the EU (Ireland).
  • You can delete your account yourself at any time: My account → Delete my account.

1. Who runs Motoropolis Online

Motoropolis Online (torqueblueprint.com/online/) is a free, non-commercial browser game made by the creator of the TorqueBlueprint YouTube channel ("we", "us"). It is a hobby fan project. It is not made, endorsed or sponsored by Google or YouTube.

We decide what data the game uses and why, so we are responsible for it (the "data controller"). Contact: torqueblueprint@gmail.com.

Motoropolis Online uses YouTube API Services. By signing in you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles its part under the Google Privacy Policy.

3. How we use your YouTube data

When you sign in, Google asks you to allow "View your YouTube account" (the youtube.readonly permission). We use it for one thing only: right after you sign in, our server makes a single request to the YouTube Data API (channels.list, "my channel") to learn which channel you are, then saves the channel title, the channel picture link and the fingerprint described above. Your title and picture are refreshed the next time you sign in. Our server does not keep the Google access token.

The permission is read-only: it cannot upload, delete, comment, subscribe or change anything on YouTube.

Motoropolis Online's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In practice this means: we use Google and YouTube data only to sign you in and show your channel in the game; we never use it for advertising; we never sell it; we never use it to train AI or machine-learning models; and no person reads it unless you ask us to (for example, for support), it is needed for security or abuse, or the law requires it.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell, rent or share your data with advertisers or data brokers. Right now other players cannot see your profile. If we ever add public features (for example a leaderboard showing channel names), we will update this policy and tell you in the game first.

The game runs on a few service providers, who process data only to provide their service:

  • Supabase (database, sign-in and server functions). Data is stored in the European Union, in Ireland (AWS eu-west-1).
  • Google (Google sign-in, YouTube Data API; channel pictures are loaded from Google's image servers).
  • GitHub Pages (hosts the web page), jsDelivr (delivers the open-source code libraries the page uses) and Google Fonts (fonts). Like any website, these see your IP address when your browser downloads files from them.

We may disclose data if the law requires it.

5. Legal basis (EU/UK GDPR)

  • Running the game you signed up for (contract): account, YouTube channel link, game data.
  • Legitimate interests: security, preventing cheating and duplicate accounts (the channel fingerprint), server logs.
  • Your consent on Google's screen for the YouTube permission. You can withdraw it at any time (see section 7).

6. How long we keep data

  • Account and game data: as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, it is erased from our database straight away.
  • Our hosting provider's automatic backups (if any on our plan) are kept for a short, fixed period and then overwritten. We never restore a deleted account from a backup.
  • Server logs: kept by our providers for a limited period under their own policies.
  • Seasons: game data (balance, history, jobs) may be reset for everyone when a new Season starts. We will announce it in advance.
  • Your channel title and picture are refreshed every time you sign in. If you don't sign in for 30 days, we remove them (the game shows your initials) until you sign in again.

7. Delete your account, or remove our access

  • In the game: sign in, open My account, choose Delete my account and type DELETE. Your profile, channel name, picture, fingerprint, balance, history, job, salary days and sign-in record are deleted at once. This cannot be undone. You can sign up again later, starting from $0.
  • By email: write to torqueblueprint@gmail.com. We may ask you to confirm the account is yours. We will delete it within 7 days.
  • Remove our access to your Google account: go to your Google security settings (third-party access) and remove "Motoropolis Online". This stops any further access; to also erase what we already store, delete your account as above or email us.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your data, or object to or restrict how we use it. Email torqueblueprint@gmail.com and we will reply within one month. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

Motoropolis Online is not meant for children under 13. You must be old enough to have your own YouTube channel and to agree to these terms yourself in your country (in some countries that is 14, 15 or 16). If you think a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete the account.

10. Security

All connections use HTTPS. Each player can only read their own rows in the database, money and jobs can only change through checked server functions, and your channel ID is stored only as a one-way fingerprint. No system is perfectly secure, but if something goes wrong we will tell affected players and the authorities as the law requires.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If the change is important (for example, new data or a new use), we will tell you in the game before it applies.
